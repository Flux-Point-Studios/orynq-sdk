Bound connection readiness and receipt/anchor submission with optional timeouts, and make the retry contract exact. A node refusal (1010/1011/1014) or a failure before broadcast is `not-landed`, keeping the node's message verbatim and the original error as `cause`; anchors are now signed before they are sent. The pool alone judges validity: a dry-run applies on the best block's own state and reported `exhaustsResources` whenever the runtime filled that block with mandatory weight, so its validity verdict no longer stops a receipt. After a lost watch, storage is re-read once per block until the deadline before reporting `inclusion-unknown`, which carries the transaction hash, signer, nonce, mortality end, last head read and cause. The new `resolveInclusion()` settles such an error from finalized storage as `landed` or `not-landed`. A stored record with the same payload is success (`reconciled: true`) and is checked before broadcasting, so a retry never pays for a duplicate. `waitForCertification` survives a dropped or stalled connection within its budget, and `submitCertifiedReceipt` reports a failed wait after the receipt landed as `MateriosCertificationError` carrying the submit result. An unresponsive connection is replaced before the next submission, `disconnect()` aborts a pending `connect()` without rethrowing, and connection errors keep their cause.
